Applications

The research in robotics are typically focusing on specific problems such as design, planning and control. The study of integrated robotic system is usually not so appreciated and is taken for granted most often. However, to make robots useful for real-world problems, it is extremely important to integrate robotic components from different levels in terms of hardware design, task and motion planning, perception, execution etc. To facilitate this process, we are developing an intelligent industrial robot operation system - CobotSys at COBOT Technology which allows the end-users to check the validity or the optimality for the design, planning, perception and execution processes without really building most of the hardware setups beforehand. We will report many of our recent progress about this work in the future.

On this page, some real-world applications from my past research will be presented ranging from robust grasping to compliant manipulation, as well as deep learning based object defects inspection. From my personal experience, it is always important to bear in mind that robotics is great for the world, however, it is challenging to make robots work for the real world applications.

Current Applications and Industry Translation (2025–2026)

Our applied research connects robotic grasping, tactile sensing and dexterous manipulation with real-world systems. Recent work spans humanoid robots, tactile data acquisition, healthcare-oriented robotics and scientific automation.

Tianwen Humanoid Robot — From Interaction to Real-world Manipulation

The Tianwen humanoid platform has been used to explore stable physical interaction, tool handling and service-oriented manipulation. Public demonstrations have included handshaking, holding laboratory tools, grasping cups and serving coffee. These scenarios provide practical testbeds for our work on dexterous hands, visuo-tactile perception, VLA-based task understanding and closed-loop manipulation. [Tianwen laboratory report] [Coffee-shop demonstration]

HandX Innovation — Tactile Data and Healthcare Robotics

HandX Innovation, founded in 2024, focuses on translating manipulation research into practical embodied-intelligence systems, including tactile data collection, humanoid robot applications and healthcare-oriented robots.

HandX Fabric Tactile Data Glove

The HandX fabric tactile glove is designed as a lightweight data interface for imitation learning and embodied manipulation. Public product reports describe 45 standard tactile sensing points with optional expansion to 800 points, together with synchronized tactile, camera and motion-tracking streams. [Product launch report]

Robotic Moxibustion

HandX's robotic moxibustion systems combine 3D body perception, target positioning and repeatable motion control to reproduce common moxibustion trajectories. Public reports show the robots operating in experience stores, exhibitions and healthcare-related demonstration settings. The images below show the actual systems in use; they illustrate engineering deployment rather than clinical efficacy. [Optics Valley report] [Exhibition report]

Other Application Directions

  • Industrial grasping, picking and manufacturing. Earlier work includes vision-guided picking, robust grasping, flexible manipulation, machine-vision inspection and factory automation.
  • AI for Science and automated laboratories. An emerging direction is embodied manipulation for scientific experiments, especially repeatable wet-lab operations and multimodal scientific-operation data collection.
  • Medical and physical-interaction robotics. Research includes robotic ultrasound, compliant physical interaction and other human-facing robotic systems.
