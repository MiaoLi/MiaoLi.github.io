About

My research interests are in robotics, embodied robot learning, machine learning and applied nonlinear control. They encompass robot learning and control, object grasping and manipulation, human-robot interaction, robotic hands and tactile sensing, and neuroscience. Recent work follows three linked research themes: reliable grasping, dexterous manipulation, and collaborative robotic systems. I am particularly interested in finding the deep connections between dynamics of intelligent systems and learning algorithms (learning from humans or from optimizations), which enables adaptive, efficient and robust control design for complex systems. The goal of my research is to enable robots to perform skills with the level of dexterity and flexibility that human demonstrates in similar tasks. *I am also particularlly interested in finding the novel application of robotic grasping and manipulation in the real world.*

Research and Impact Highlights: My group's work centers on three questions: grasp reliably (grasp planning, tactile perception and adaptive control), manipulate with skill (contact-rich dexterous action, imitation learning and embodied AI), and work together (human–robot interaction and multi-robot collaboration). Our research has progressed from robotic hands and industrial picking to the Tianwen/Yuanyou humanoid platforms. Recent highlights include the 2026 World Humanoid Robot Games gold medal in autonomous dexterous powder weighing, AAAI 2026 RGMP on geometric-prior multimodal policy learning, and a contribution to the 2025 Red Dot award-winning dexterous hand. Beyond academia, I founded COBOT Technology (2016) and HandX Innovation (2024), connecting robotic research to industrial automation, tactile data collection and healthcare-related robotics. My work has also been recognized with the 2019 Hubei May 1st Labor Medal and the 2020 Innovation & Entrepreneurship Talents Award.

Short Bio: I am a professor in the School of Robotics at Wuhan University. I received my doctoral training at the Learning Algorithms and Systems Laboratory (LASA) at EPFL, Switzerland, working with Professor Aude Billard on dynamic grasp adaptation from humans to robots. Before that, I studied at Huazhong University of Science and Technology, China. I also co-founded COBOT Technology in 2016 and founded HandX Innovation in 2024, translating research on robotic manipulation into real-world systems.
